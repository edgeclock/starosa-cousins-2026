Decision 3 · RJ

RJ, can you find us a rental car?

A car for Nov 28–29 would make the weekend much easier: getting to Sta. Rosa, going between the Airbnb and EK, and picking up Greta after her concert. Can you check what's available and send the options in the GC?

Useful to find out

  • Price for 2 days, and what's included (fuel, toll, insurance)
  • Seats: room for 6 people plus bags (a 7-seater is safest)
  • Self-drive or with a driver, and the rate for each
  • Where and when to pick up and return it
  • Requirements: license, ID, deposit
